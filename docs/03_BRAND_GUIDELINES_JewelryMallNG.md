# JewelryMallNG — Comprehensive Brand Document
**Version 1.0 | 30 Sep 2026 | Hiddekel Studio | Applies to: website, WhatsApp, social, packaging (future)**

## 1. Brand Essence
- **Promise:** "Everyday luxury you can verify — right price, real stock, human help on WhatsApp."
- **Positioning:** Nigeria's most trustworthy fashion-jewelry mall for retail sparkle and wholesale hustle. Not ultra-high-jewelry (Aurelle) nor cheap bazaar — the credible middle: beautiful, affordable, accountable.
- **Mission:** Make beautiful jewelry easy to find, fairly priced, and reliably delivered across Nigeria.
- **Vision:** The first mall Nigerian women check before any owanbe, restock, or gift.
- **Values:** Clarity (no hidden price) • Care (human handoff) • Craft (consistent finish) • Credibility (verified payment/stock).

## 2. Audience
- **Primary — Ada, 24, Lagos (retail):** IG/TikTok native, mobile-only, price-sensitive but quality-aware, fears fakes + "price in DM" + delivery scam. Wants cash-on-clarity: see price, pay verified, track.
- **Secondary — Alhaja S, 38, wholesaler (Ibadan/Onitsha):** buys 50-200 pcs/month, needs tier discount, mixed packs, receipt + packing list, pickup/dispatch reliability.
- **Tertiary — Gift buyer (male, 28-45):** needs guidance + fast delivery + gift wrap note (Phase 2).
- **Cultural notes:** owanbe/asọ-ebi seasonality, Detty December peak, church/mosque modest luxury, skin-tone inclusive imagery, Naira pricing with no dollar confusion.

## 3. Personality & Archetype
60% Lover (beauty, detail) + 30% Sage (guides, verifies) + 10% Jester (warm WhatsApp wit). Never snobbish, never pushy, never vulgar.
- **We are:** warm, precise, proud-Nigerian, reassuring.
- **We are not:** luxury-cold, slang-heavy, vague, over-promising.

## 4. Voice & Tone
- **Voice pillars:** Clear → "₦12,500 for 1-11 pcs, ₦10,800 for 12+." Warm → "Hi Ada, seen!" Helpful → next step + link always.
- **Website:** polished en-NG, short sentences, scannable. Prices always with tier + stock.
- **WhatsApp:** conversational, 1-2 lines per bubble, emoji max 1 per message (✨/💛), light Pidgin only when customer uses it ("No wahala, I don check am"). Always cite source: "From our approved list… [link]".
- **Forbidden:** "Trust me", "Cheapest ever", inventing dates, blaming customer, ALL CAPS shouting.
- **Templates:** greeting, price quote, stock check, order status (with auth), handoff ("I'm bringing in Adaeze from our team — she has our full chat, no need to repeat").

## 5. Naming & Taglines
- **Master:** JewelryMallNG (one word + NG, never "Jewellry", "Jewellery Mall"). Short: JMNG in internal/staff contexts only.
- **Tagline (primary):** *Shine, Verified.* **Alternatives:** *Real Price. Real Stock. Real Shine.* / *Retail sparkle. Wholesale muscle.*
- **Product naming:** `[Collection] [Piece] in [Finish/Colour]` e.g., "Zaria Hoops in 18k Gold Vermeil". SKUs: `JMNG-{FAM}-{VAR}-{PACK}`.

## 6. Logo System
- **Primary:** wordmark "JewelryMall" in high-contrast serif + "NG" in gold capsule. For header/footer.
- **Secondary (stacked):** for square avatars, favicon source, packing tape (future).
- **Monogram:** `JM` ligature inside faceted gem outline — app icon, favicon (32px), WhatsApp profile (with safe padding), social avatar.
- **Clearspace:** height of gem facet on all sides. Min sizes: digital 32px wide (mono) / 96px (primary), print 25mm.
- **Light/dark:** ink-on-ivory primary; gold-on-onyx reversed for footer/preloader.
- **Misuse (never):** stretch, recolour, drop shadow, busy photo behind, thin-outline at small size, "price in DM" lockup.
- **Files to deliver:** SVG, PDF print, PNG 1x/2x/3x, ICO, social 1080×1080 + highlight covers.

## 7. Colour Palette (approved, WCAG AA)
Inspired by gold on deep onyx + ivory — premium but warm, works on low-brightness Android.
- **Onyx `#121014`** — text/footer/BG dark. **Ivory `#FAF7F1`** — page BG.
- **Gold `#C6A15B`** (primary) / **Deep Gold `#9A7B2E`** (hover/text on light) / **Champagne `#F1E3C3`** (tint for badges).
- **Emerald `#0E5E4A`** — wholesale/success/verified checks.
- **Blush `#F6D9D0`** — retail accents, sale tint (text always Onyx on it).
- **Clay `#B4432F`** — error/low-stock only.
- **Stone palette:** `#E8E2D6` borders, `#6E675C` muted text, `#FFFFFF` cards.
- **Usage ratio:** 60 Ivory/white, 25 Onyx, 10 Gold, 5 Emerald/Blush. Gold never for body text <14px (use Deep Gold). Contrast: Onyx on Ivory 15.8:1, Deep Gold on Ivory 4.6:1 ✅.
- **Tailwind tokens:** see Design System doc.

## 8. Typography
- **Display:** Fraunces (or Playfair Display fallback) — headlines, prices on PDP hero, campaign. 600-700, tight -1%.
- **Body/UI:** Inter — everything else, tabular numbers for prices (`font-feature: tnum`).
- **WhatsApp/social caption:** system font, sentence case.
- **Scale:** Display 40/32/28, H1 32, H2 24, H3 20, Body 16/14, Caption 12. Line-height 1.2 display, 1.6 body. Prices always 20+ semibold.
- **Never:** more than 2 families, all-caps body, thin serif <16px.

## 9. Imagery & Iconography
- **Photo style:** warm daylight, deep-brown skin priority, macro clasp/finish detail, flat-lay on ivory linen + one lifestyle (owambe/street). No over-whitening, no plastic blur. Every variant must have ≥3 images: front, worn, detail/scale (coin/hand).
- **Image-match rule:** catalogue shots on neutral #EEE background for AI accuracy; lifestyle kept separate gallery.
- **Icons:** 1.5px stroke rounded (Lucide), gold for value, emerald for verified, clay for alert. No filled emoji as UI icon.
- **Pattern:** subtle faceted-gem line pattern at 6% opacity for hero/footer only.

## 10. Applications
- **Website:** ivory BG, onyx header (sticky, gold underline on active), gold CTA (see Design System), emerald "Verified stock/price" ticks, champagne tier-price table.
- **WhatsApp:** profile = monogram on onyx, bio: "JewelryMallNG ✨ Real price • Real stock • Mon–Sat 9-6 | jewelrymallng.com", greeting template with catalogue link.
- **Social:** avatar = monogram, bio link = `/r/ig` short link (tracked), highlight covers: Shop, Wholesale, Reviews, FAQ, Order Track (onyx + gold glyphs).
- **Future packaging (not in scope, spec for later):** ivory rigid box, onyx sleeve, gold foil mono, thank-you card with WhatsApp QR + review incentive.

## 11. Brand Governance
- Owner: JewelryMallNG decision-maker approves; Hiddekel applies to web. Guide versioned (`brand-v1.0`). Any new colour/font needs written approval. Staff must pass 30-min brand quiz at training. Review quarterly or before Detty December push.

## 12. Competitor Separation
vs generic IG vendors: we show price + stock + verified payment (they hide). vs Jumia/Konga: we specialize (curated jewelry + WhatsApp concierge). vs high-jewelry: we are accessible luxury, not intimidation.
